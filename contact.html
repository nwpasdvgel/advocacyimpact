Contact Us

Regional and global human rights forums welcome contributions from specialized NGOs, researchers, and civil society networks.

We warmly welcome small and medium-sized NGOs, individual researchers, and large organizations looking to take their engagement with the United Nations system to the next level. Whether you are just getting started or aiming to strengthen ongoing initiatives, we are ready to explore your perspectives, opportunities, and ambitions—and help you turn them into concrete, meaningful action. Get in touch to start shaping your strategy, book a consultation, or plan your next intervention.

Multilingual Team

Our multilingual team offers professional support in English, Spanish, and French, serving both international teams and local organizations that need multilingual guidance to communicate effectively with their target audiences.

Through simultaneous interpretation, translation, and facilitation, we combine in-house expertise with a global network of capable language professionals. We have organized and managed interpretation during UN side events, as well as in high-profile venues such as Conference Room 4 in New York, and in Geneva and Nairobi.

Language is not only about words; it conveys culture, nuance, and connection. Providing accurate and context-sensitive language support is an integral part of our communications services, enabling organizations to maximize their impact at the UN and other international forums.

Contact us to explore how we can help your organization communicate, connect, and influence at a global scale.
